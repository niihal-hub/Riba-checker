# Halal Alternatives
When a document is flagged for riba, gharar, or maysir, these are the common Sharia-compliant alternatives.

## For Loans
- Murabaha (Cost-Plus-Profit): The seller buys the asset and sells it to the buyer at a markup. The price is fixed and does not increase over time.
- Ijarah (Leasing): The owner leases an asset for a fee.
- Musharakah/Mudarabah (Profit-Sharing): Partners share both the profits and the losses.
- Qard Hassan (Benevolent Loan): An interest-free loan.

## For Insurance
- Takaful: A mutual cooperative system where members contribute to a fund to help each other.

## For Savings/Investments
- Sharia-compliant funds that avoid interest-bearing bonds and invest in ethical equities.

Sources: AAOIFI, IFSB.
