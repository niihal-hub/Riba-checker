# Riba al-Nasia (Time-based Interest)
Riba al-Nasia is the most common form of interest. It is the increase charged on a loan based on the length of time the money is borrowed.

## Core Principle
Money is seen as a medium of exchange, not a commodity to be sold for more money. Any loan that requires a predetermined increase to be paid back is considered riba.

## Common Indicators
- Fixed interest rates (e.g., 5% APR).
- Compounding interest (interest on interest).
- Late fees that increase the principal debt.

## Scholarly Views
- Mainstream view: Strictly forbidden (Haram).
- Some modern debates: Differ on whether administrative costs (flat fees) are riba if they don't scale with the loan amount.

Sources: AAOIFI, Classical Fiqh.
