# Maysir (Gambling/Speculation)
Maysir is any transaction where one party gains at the expense of another based on chance, rather than productive economic activity.

## Core Principle
Wealth should not be acquired through gambling or games of chance.

## Common Indicators
- Contracts where the outcome is purely luck-based.
- High-risk derivatives or speculative trades where the goal is betting on price movements rather than owning an asset.
- Lottery-based financial products.

## Scholarly Views
- Mainstream view: Strictly forbidden.
- Differ on: Certain types of options trading or high-leverage forex (often viewed as maysir).

Sources: AAOIFI, Classical Fiqh.
